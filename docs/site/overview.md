# BOT Chain overview

BOT Chain is an EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain built for AI agents, DePIN networks and decentralized applications. You build with Ethereum tools, with fast finality and low transaction fees.

### Technology {#technology}

- Block time: 0.75 seconds. Average finality: about 0.9 seconds.
- EVM compatible: use Solidity, MetaMask, Hardhat, Foundry, ethers.js and web3.js with minimal changes.
- Consensus: a hybrid design called SPoA that combines physical compute-backed authority with a high-performance staking consensus.
- Security: BFT logic with slashing for double-signing or downtime.
- Validators secure the network through BOT staking, and DePIN nodes contribute physical compute.

### BOT token {#bot-token}

BOT is the native token of BOT Chain and is used to pay on-chain gas fees.

### Ecosystem {#ecosystem}

- **BO Wallet**: a Web3 gateway that supports EOA and MPC, with DePIN compute power and POS staking rewards.
- **BOT Bridge**: a bridge connecting major L1 ecosystems.
- **B DEX**: the first decentralized exchange on BOT Chain for trading tokens, providing liquidity and liquidity mining.
- **BOT Explorer**: view transactions, addresses, contracts and network activity.
