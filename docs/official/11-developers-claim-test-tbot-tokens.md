# Claim test tBOT Tokens

### Claim tBOT from Online Faucet {#claim-tbot-from-online-faucet}

Follow these steps to claim test tBOT on BOT Chain Testnet:

1.  Switch your wallet to **BOT Chain Testnet** (Chain ID: **968**).
    
2.  Open [https://faucet.botchain.ai/basic](https://faucet.botchain.ai/basic).
    
3.  Enter your wallet address and complete verification.
    
4.  After claiming, click the returned **tx hash** to view the transaction on [https://scan.bohr.life/](https://scan.bohr.life/).
    

**Limits:** Each address can claim up to **10 tBOT every 24 hours**. tBOT has **no real-world value**.
